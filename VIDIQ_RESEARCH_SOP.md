# vidIQ & 9 Figure Buddy Keyword Research SOP

This guide provides the exact keyword discovery and research strategy for launching new offers, giveaways, and products through the Offer Launchpad, 9 Figure Buddy, and ViralForge AI.

---

## 1. The Core Rule of vidIQ Search
> **Never search the brand name or product title.** Nobody searches for unbranded names yet. Instead, search what people are struggling with, the outcome they want, or the expensive alternative they hate paying for.

---

## 2. The 4-Bucket Framework

Whenever preparing a new offer, fill out these 4 buckets:

### Bucket 1: The Urgent Pain / Frustration
* **Question:** What is frustrating the target buyer right now?
* **Examples:**
  * Affiliate/Traffic: `zero clicks on link`, `stop cold DMing`, `getting banned on facebook`, `why my links don't work`
  * SaaS/Creator: `no views on youtube shorts`, `running out of content ideas`, `200 view jail`
  * Education: `teacher burnout sunday`, `grading papers all weekend`
  * Parenting: `toddler screen time battle`, `kids won't go to sleep`

### Bucket 2: The Desired Outcome (The "How To")
* **Question:** What specific result do they wish happened today?
* **Examples:**
  * `how to get free traffic to affiliate links`
  * `how to build an email list from scratch`
  * `how to write lesson plans fast`
  * `how to make kids love reading`
  * `passive income for beginners 2026`

### Bucket 3: The Broad Vehicle / Tool Category
* **Question:** What category does the viewer think they need?
* **Examples:**
  * `free traffic sources`
  * `secret websites for side hustles`
  * `lead capture page builder free`
  * `ai lesson planner for teachers`
  * `best ai tools for content creators`

### Bucket 4: Competitor Comparisons & Expensive Alternatives
* **Question:** What expensive tool or alternative are they already familiar with?
* **Examples:**
  * `clickfunnels alternative free`
  * `solo ads review 2026`
  * `systeme io vs clickfunnels`
  * `chatgpt prompts for teachers`

---

## 3. The vidIQ Verification Loop
1. Enter your 2-to-3-word seed phrase into vidIQ's **Keyword Inspector**.
2. Filter for:
   * **Overall Score:** 60+ (Green Zone)
   * **Search Volume:** Medium to High
   * **Competition:** Low to Medium
3. Check **Trending / Rising** modifiers:
   * `...for beginners`
   * `...without spending money`
   * `...step by step`

---

Once you find the high-scoring vidIQ phrase:
1. Open [https://traffic.aisimplesystem.com/launchpad](https://traffic.aisimplesystem.com/launchpad)
